Annex privacy policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Annex consists of an Android app and a Mac app made by Classroom Adventure. This policy explains what they do with your information.

Information we collect

We don't collect any personal information. Annex has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own.

How the screen and input travel

The Mac app sends its extra display as video straight to your Android device, over a USB cable or your local Wi-Fi network. Touch and stylus input go back the same way. Nothing passes through the internet or through us.

Information stored on your devices

The Android app creates a random device ID and keeps it on the device. When you approve the device on your Mac, the Mac app stores that ID and the device's name so it doesn't ask again. You can remove them any time: “Ask the Mac for approval again” in the Android app's settings, or “Forget Approved Devices” in the Mac app's menu.

Display settings such as size and frame rate are stored only on the device.

Permissions

On Android, Annex uses network access to find your Mac and receive the display. On the Mac, Screen Recording is used only to capture Annex's own extra display, and Accessibility is used only to turn your touches into pointer movement.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
