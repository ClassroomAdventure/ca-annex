# Google Play listing (English)

## App name (30)
Annex – Second screen for Mac

## Short description (80)
Use your Android tablet, phone or foldable as an extra display for your Mac.

## Full description (4000)
Annex turns your Android device into a real second display for your Mac — like Sidecar, but for Android.

Your Mac sees a new monitor. Drag windows onto it, arrange it next to your Mac's screen in System Settings, and use touch or a stylus to click, drag and scroll.

• Wi-Fi or USB — join the same Wi-Fi and your Mac shows up on its own, or plug in a USB cable and it starts automatically.
• Low latency — about 16 ms from the Mac to your screen over Wi-Fi. The pointer is drawn on your device, so it stays right under your finger.
• Sharp — Retina rendering at your device's native resolution, with larger and smaller size options.
• Made for foldables — fold, unfold or rotate and the Mac display resizes to match.
• Smooth when it moves — up to 120 Hz on supported screens, only while something is moving, to save battery.
• Private — the screen goes straight from your Mac to your device. No accounts, no ads, no servers. New devices must be approved on the Mac before they can connect.
• Free and open source (MIT).

Requirements
• The free Annex app for Mac (macOS 14 or later): classroomadventure.github.io/ca-annex
• Android 8.0 or later
• For USB: USB debugging on the device, and Android platform tools on the Mac

No Mac nearby? Tap “Try it without a Mac” to try the controls in demo mode.
